How to use this Jeopardy! game

  1. Open this file in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on your Mac (double-click it). It needs no internet. In Adobe Connect, share your screen or the browser window. Press F11 / Ctrl+Cmd+F for full screen.
  2. Teams: click + Add team, rename it in the box, type student names and press Enter. Drag a name to another team (or pick a team in its dropdown). ✕ removes a student; Delete team removes the team. Or paste a roster and click Shuffle into teams.
  3. Questions: drop a CSV on the dashed box, click Choose file…, or click Use built-in cross-curricular sample. Then click Start game.
  4. Play: click a dollar amount. Read the clue, click Show Answer, then click ✓ (adds the value) or ✗ (subtracts it) for any team. You can mark several teams (one wrong, another steals). Click Back to board (or press Esc); the square goes blank. Nobody / Back to board leaves scores alone.
  5. Daily Double: pick the team and type its wager (up to its score, or up to the top board value if it has less). Only that team is scored.
  6. Final Jeopardy: click Final Jeopardy on the right of the score bar. Reveal the category, type each team's wager, reveal the clue (30-second thinking music and timer), show the answer, tick right/wrong for each team, then Apply wagers.
  7. Scores: +/− buttons under each team change the score by 100 (click the team name to type an exact score). Hover a team name to see its members. Undo reverses the last award.
  8. Everything is saved in this browser automatically, so a refresh will not lose the game. Setup returns to team setup; New game resets scores and the board (teams stay).
  9. Sound: 🔊 mutes, the slider sets volume. Sounds are original, not the TV show's audio.

CSV format

First row is the header. Columns: category,value,clue,answer,daily_double. Only category, clue and answer are required. Up to 6 categories × 5 clues, in the order they first appear.

  • value is optional: blank values become 200, 400, 600, 800, 1000 by row order within the category.
  • daily_double: yes marks a Daily Double (any number of them).
  • Final Jeopardy: one row whose category is final or final:Category Name.
  • Put fields with commas, quotes or line breaks in double quotes; write a quote as "". Wrap code in backticks (`df.head()`) to show it in code font.
